Welcome to HoneyBound-Web

A browser-based TOTP authenticator that uses WebAuthn-backed device credentials to protect locally stored account secrets and support optional honeyseed detection for linked services.

Linked Phone Setup:
This device was paired from your laptop. Initialize the authenticator here and HoneyBound will import the accounts that already exist on your laptop before opening the dashboard.
Security Features:
WebAuthn platform credentials with user verification
Local secret encryption with optional PRF-assisted flows
Optional honeyseed export for linked services
RFC 6238 compliant TOTP generation
